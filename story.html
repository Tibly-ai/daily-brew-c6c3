Our Heritage & Philosophy

Sown in Shade,
Served in Light.

The Daily Brew began with a simple desire: to gather people inside a glass canopy where slow mornings feel permanent and coffee honors the soil it left behind.

We source all our coffees through strict direct-trade relationships, paying premium rates directly to farm owners in Central America, East Africa, and Sumatra. By bypassing traditional brokers, we ensure our partner growers can invest back into sustainable agricultural practices, organic fertilizers, and living wages for their harvest teams.

Each bean is roasted in collaboration with regional micro-roasters who share our standard for clarity and flavor. Roasted in small batches, we profile every single-origin variety to showcase the specific altitude, mineral profile, and climate of its original orchard.

The Glasshouse Haven

Our cafe resides within a restored architectural greenhouse, filled with over sixty species of tropical plants. We believe clean air, quiet foliage, and natural light elevate the sensory experience of enjoying exceptional coffee.
