We believe coffee is best appreciated in a setting that mirrors the soil it grew from.

Step out of the busy city streets and into our micro-greenhouse. Designed with soaring glass panes and packed with curated botanical life, our space offers a serene refuge where you can gather your thoughts, connect with friends, and enjoy extraordinary coffee roasted with a gentle hand.

Every single-origin roast on our rotating list is purchased directly from ethical farming cooperatives. We select and roast these small batches with clean, bright flavor profiles in mind, preserving the distinct regional fruit, floral, and spice notes of the coffee cherries.

Our Space

Under a Glass Canopy

Flooded with natural sunlight, our shop houses a living collection of rare tropical ferns, towering fiddle leaf figs, and delicate orchids. The environment naturally purifies the air, helping you breathe deep and relax with your morning brew.

Sourcing Philosophy

Traceable Single-Origins

We build direct-relationship agreements with family farms and small-scale growing co-ops in Central America, East Africa, and Southeast Asia. By paying well above fair-trade market minimums, we secure the finest harvests and support sustainable rural agriculture.
